Nancy and Sonia._

Thank you for helping us test Sokana360! This is a **test site**, so please use
made-up information for any client details. Use your real name and email for
your own doula account only. It should take about 20 minutes.

If you get stuck, write down what you were doing and email
**hello@sokanacollective.com**. A screenshot really helps.

---

## Part 1: Accept your invite

1. Look for an email titled **"You're Invited to Set Up Your Sokana360
   Account."** Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see it.
2. Click the **Set up my account** link in the email.
3. Create and confirm your password. Use at least 8 characters, and don't reuse
   a password from another site.
4. Log in with the **same email address the invite was sent to** and your new
   password.
5. If you're asked to verify your email, open the verification email and click
   the link, then log in again.

✅ You're done with Part 1 when you see your doula dashboard.

## Part 2: Update your profile

Click the **Profile** tab.

1. **Basic info:** first and last name. Your address is at the bottom of the
   Profile tab.
2. **About me (bio):** _required._ Write 3 to 5 sentences **in first person**,
   the way you'd introduce yourself to a family. Example: _"I'm a birth and
   postpartum doula serving the South Side of Chicago. I believe every family
   deserves…"_ Families will see this.
3. **Scheduling link:** paste your Calendly or Google booking page link, if you
   have one, so families can book time with you. Set your **availability
   status** to Available, or to Unavailable with the dates you'll be away.
4. **Certifications & training:** in the header beside your photo and name, add
   each certification, with its name, the organization that issued it, and the
   year.
5. **Pronouns and demographics:** these are **optional**. Share only what you're
   comfortable with.
6. Click **Save Changes**.
7. Upload a profile photo if you'd like, since families see it next to your bio.

## Part 3: Upload your documents

Click the **Documents** tab and upload PDF, JPG, or PNG files (10 MB max each).

| Document                        | Required?                                      |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Doula training certificate      | **Required**                                   |
| Liability insurance             | Optional. _Required for Medicaid clients._     |
| Illinois Medicaid certification | Optional                                       |
| Miscellaneous documents         | Optional (anything else you'd like us to have) |

You will **not** be asked for a W9, direct deposit form, or background check
here.

## Part 4: Mark your profile complete

1. Go back to **Profile** and scroll to the bottom.
2. Click **Mark profile complete.** The button only works once your required
   fields and your Doula training certificate are in.
3. You'll see **"Submitted for review."** The Sokana team gets a notice and will
   review your profile and documents.
4. Your account becomes fully active once the team approves your information.
